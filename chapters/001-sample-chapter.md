This is placeholder text so you can see how a chapter looks. Replace this file with your own chapter, or add new files next to it: `002-your-title.md`, `003-another.md`, and so on. Files are listed in filename order.

Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. Write *italics* with single asterisks and **bold** with double asterisks.

"Dialogue reads cleanly on its own line," she said, and the page gives it room to breathe.

* * *

A line containing only `* * *` (or `***`) becomes a scene break, drawn as a small centred ornament.

> Lines starting with `>` become an indented passage — useful for letters, signs, or a Pokédex entry.

## A heading inside a chapter

Use `##` for a section heading within a chapter if you need one. The chapter's own title comes from the `title:` line at the top of the file, so you don't need to repeat it.
